    Implementation¶

    How the models are built in code, computed and sampled.

    Code¶

    The model extends PyRenew, pinned to a commit, and runs in JAX with NumPyro. Its code is in bdbvhubrenewal.model and the variants are in forecasters.renewal. The model is configured by ModelConfig.

    PyRenew and what is built here¶

    Component PyRenew piece Built here
    Pooled \(R_p(t)\) BaseSubpopulationInfections, WeeklyTemporalProcess NonCentredRandomWalk, for the frozen seeding weeks, the separate forecast steps and their reversion.
    Renewal with importation and depletion BaseSubpopulationInfections, subclassed coupled_renewal, called from _infections_from_rt, and Importation, which gives the mixing matrix as PyRenew's mobility_matrix_rv does.
    Ascertainment \(\alpha(s)\) IndependentAscertainment with a temporal process None.
    Onset-to-report delay \(f_{p,s}(d)\) None HazardDelay, with province offsets and drifts from pooled_walk, as the Rt deviations.
    Missing onsets \(m_p(t)\) None MissingOnsetFraction, a pooled walk of the same form as \(R_p\) with its own parameters.
    Known and imputed onsets BaseObservationProcess with temporal_pmf_rv for the infection-to-onset pmf (the incubation period, a CensoredPMF with a random mean and SD), NegativeBinomialNoise, compute_prop_already_reported for the publication lag OnsetStreams.
    Publication lag None A Dirichlet simplex fitted to consecutive releases by ReleasePairLikelihood.
    Delays of known-onset cases None DirichletMultinomialNoise, a noise model in the form of PyRenew's CountNoise for counts by delay given weekly totals.
    BVDOutbreakSize Rt None RtPseudoObservation, a RandomVariable the latent process calls, as it holds both \(R\) and infections.

    PyRenew pieces that give a different model, sample less well, or would only rename one line, are not used.

    • The RandomWalk temporal process puts the step SD in the step distribution, so it is centred in the SD.
    • ARProcess gives the same walk, log density and gradient, but NUTS needs more tree steps with it, so the walk samples its steps as one vectorised site.
    • AR1 draws its first state from the stationary distribution, and DifferencedAR1 is AR(1) on the differences. Here walks start at a fixed value and stay there over the seeding weeks.
    • SubpopulationInfections samples one deviation process per province and subtracts their mean, which leaves a direction the data do not identify. Here the \(P_s - 1\) coordinates on the sum-to-zero basis \(Q\) of the seeded provinces are sampled.
    • HierarchicalNormalPrior has no sum-to-zero constraint. On the basis coordinates it is the same prior, but it would replace one sample statement.
    • GammaGroupSdPrior gives each province its own SD with a floor, where here the provinces share one half-normal SD.
    • CountObservation and SubpopulationCounts take one fixed delay pmf and one truncation pmf. Here the delay varies with onset day, and known and imputed onsets are separate streams.
    • MobilitySubpopulationInfections uses a fixed mobility matrix, \(R\) by location and depletion capped at the susceptibles. Here a share of transmission is sent from each origin through a fixed gravity kernel, and depletion is exponential in the pool \(\nu\).

    PyRenew has no delay that varies with time, no Dirichlet-multinomial count and no pseudo-observation of \(R\).

    Baseline models¶

    The baselines are in forecasters.randomwalk and forecasters.chain, on a JAX port of baselinenowcast 0.2.2 in bdbvhubrenewal.baselinenowcast. The port is checked against fixtures from the R package, and has one named extension, the chain ladder of release snapshots, which skips unobserved cells and fits one daily delay shared by pairs of any gap. The models use the port's chain ladder on snapshots. The observation model is in forecasters.obs, and fits and draws its new reports with the port's fit_by_horizon, fit_nb and sample_nb.

    Part Where
    Snapshots as arrays [location, release, day] data.snapshots
    Pair sums, the daily delay fit and growth baselinenowcast.snapshots, pair sums jit with locations on the leading axis
    Own or pooled source of each estimate forecasters.pooling
    New reports on the count to come forecasters.obs
    One random walk for both models forecasters.walk
    Introductions into places with no recent case forecasters.introductions
    Zone samples that sum to the province samples forecasters.zones
    Backtest on real releases evaluation.nowcast

    Shapes are fixed within an origin, so each new origin compiles once. Pandas only builds the inputs and the output frames.

    Numerics¶

    The model runs in single precision (float32), JAX's default.

    • Depletion is carried on \(\log S\), which needs no subtraction, and \(1 - e^{-y}\) is computed with expm1.
    • The generation interval, the incubation period and the hub's imputation delay are double interval censored in closed form, \(G(d + 1) - 2G(d) + G(d - 1)\) with the integrated cdf (model.censored). The generation interval is computed once and the hub's delay once per release, both in float64, and the incubation period in float32 at each draw of its mean and SD (CensoredPMF); all match primarycensored.
    • The simulator's delays use the same closed form, truncated at their maximum delay and renormalised as primarycensored's \(D\). In float64 they are within \(10^{-8}\) of primarycensored, and in float32 within \(4 \times 10^{-5}\) of numerical integration.
    • The negative binomial size is kept below 1000, which is Poisson for these counts, and the Dirichlet-multinomial concentration below \(10^4\) (Likelihood).
    • The hub imputation sums are products of banded \(7 \times 7k\) blocks for each onset week, so the cost grows with the number of weeks and not its square.

    The tests check the renewal and the imputation against a NumPy reference, tests/model/numpy_reference.py.

    Sampling¶

    PyRenew's PyrenewBuilder builds each model as a MultiSignalModel, and NumPyro's NUTS fits it. Chains run vectorised unless a fit's settings say otherwise. Stored fits run their chains in parallel. Without other settings, NUTS runs 4 chains of 1000 warm-up and 1000 draws, with target acceptance 0.9 and maximum tree depth 10. Each chain starts from its own draw from the prior. The fit store's fits, which the backtest and the submission read, take each model's settings from its candidate in ci/policy.toml. renewal, renewalsp and renewalbvd use 2 chains of 500 warm-up and 750 draws, at target acceptance 0.9 (0.95 for renewalbvd). Leave-one-province-out fits take theirs from [sampler] in config/backtest.toml when BDBV_RENEWAL_SAMPLER is unset. Each fit reports its largest R-hat over parameters and the share of draws at the maximum tree depth.

    The parameterisation is as follows.

    • Every walk, the pooled \(\log R(t)\) included, samples its steps non-centred, as standard normals scaled by the step SD.
    • Province deviations sum to zero through an orthonormal basis \(Q\) of the sum-to-zero subspace of the seeded provinces, so the \(P_s - 1\) free processes are unconstrained.
    • The starting offsets of the \(\log R\) deviations are sampled centred, and those of the missing-onset fraction non-centred.
    • Walk steps after the fit axis are not sampled by NUTS. They are drawn from the prior given each posterior draw when forecasting.

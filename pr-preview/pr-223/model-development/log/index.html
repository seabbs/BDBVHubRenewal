    2026-10-30 model randomwalknowcast ages release weeks by release date and leaves out weeks ending after a release; development factors are fitted to the growth between any consecutive releases; the change still to come is Skellam with a spread fitted by age to retrospective nowcasts, and totals are floored at zero. Sam's decisions. Negative changes between releases occur at 1 to 2 of 8 to 13 points per age from 5 weeks old. The fitted Skellam has Pearson dispersion 0.97 on the retrospective errors (Poisson difference without the extra spread: 2 to 230), so a difference of negative binomials is not needed. National at +4 weeks from 10-24 has a 95% bound of 476 (4241 before, driven by the 5.3-fold factor the Friday release gave week ages 0). The 10-09 release mixes weeks 6 days old with weeks 0 days old at age 0, so f_0 is 1.65 at 10-24, against 3.5 between 10-17 and 10-24; the latest week is now underpredicted (10-17 origin: bias -1 at ages 0, 7 and 14 days). 25
    2026-10-30 model randomwalknowcast nowcasts on the hub's release triangle (totals by onset week and release): development factors from releases a week apart, own above 50 cases a week and pooled otherwise, and negative binomial sizes from nowcasts made at earlier releases. The delay-file nowcast is removed. Sam's decision, replacing the delay-file triangle, which missed late entries. Real data allow a release nowcast only from 10-17 (truth 10-24): there, ages 0, 7 and 14 days score national WIS 394, 64 and 9 against 42, 15 and 43 for the delay-file nowcast, overshooting the youngest weeks because the 10-09 release fell on a Friday and its latest week was barely reported (k = 0 factor 9.2). Reconstructed vintages: national nowcast WIS 19.5 against 63.3, forecast 58.2 against 248.1. Scenarios: national nowcast 28.8 against 34.0, forecast 100.7 against 88.5. At 10-24 each size rests on one release of 8 provinces. 25
    2026-10-30 model randomwalknowcast keeps the delay-file nowcast; a nowcast on the hub's release triangle waits for more weekly releases. Sam's decision (option a). With releases on 08-18, 10-09, 10-17 and 10-24 each week is seen at only about three consecutive weekly delays, so the release triangle has no complete row beyond 2 weeks and no retrospective nowcasts. baselinenowcast cannot estimate a delay or its uncertainty from it. 25
    2026-10-30 model randomwalk counts a week as complete once 95% of its final total is published, from the hub's releases (90% before); the delay-quantile fallback uses the 95th centile.     2026-10-30 model randomwalk and randomwalknowcast share one random walk (log-scale Gaussian steps, Poisson draws, spread pooled below 10 cases a week); randomwalknowcast's empirical-step walk and low-count rate walk are removed. randomwalk takes its last complete week from the hub's releases. Sam's decision. Real as-of-T national forecast WIS: randomwalk 37.5 to 35.4, randomwalknowcast 95.2 to 103.7 (90% coverage 0.56 to 0.75). RandomWalk nowcast weeks worsen slightly (national 46.3 to 49.3). On reconstructed vintages and scenarios randomwalk is worse (national forecast WIS 69.6 to 99.5 and 119.3 to 172.0): the release completeness is 3 weeks against about 2 from the delays, so its walk starts earlier, and the early reconstructed vintages give 6. Only the 10-17 real vintage has two releases a week apart before it. 25
    2026-10-30 model Both baselines pool the walk spread only for places under 10 cases a week; randomwalknowcast keeps 50 delays a week for its own delay. Sam's correction: a 50 a week limit pooled almost every province, giving small ones the narrow spread of large ones. With 10, real as-of-T province forecast 90% coverage is 0.85 for randomwalk (0.62 at 50, 0.81 with no pooling) and 0.77 for randomwalknowcast (0.69 at 50), with WIS 15.2 and 19.2 (15.0 and 17.8 at 50). 22

    Health zones¶

    Each model's nowcast and forecast in one health zone. Pick the province and the zone, or click a zone on the map. The map colours each zone by log((median + 1) / (recent + 1)) for the model and week chosen above the map. The zone review below has its own model and week. Recent is the mean count of the weeks ending 2026-10-10 and 2026-10-17, as reported at the 2026-11-08 release. The panel under the map gives the numbers for the zone picked. Katoyi (CD-NK-13) is not on the map: its code in the hub mapping does not match the boundary file. Zone boundaries are from BVDOutbreakSize at results-3486, from the Ministry of Health boundaries on the Humanitarian Data Exchange.

    Zone review¶

    One row per place with a forecast from the chosen model. Each week column gives the median and 90% interval. Ratio is log((median + 1) / (recent + 1)) for the chosen week. A ratio of 0.69 is double the recent count and -0.69 is half. Click a place to show it in the chart above.

    Delay and spread¶

    The growth to final of each onset day by its age at the latest release, and the observation model by weeks back. A place using its parent's estimate is labelled with the parent. How both are fitted is on the Baseline models page.

    The renewal models fit the onset-to-report delay with the cases (delays). Each row is one fit at its latest origin, and each model fits one delay for all provinces. The three renewal models give nearly the same delay, so their lines overlap. The left panel gives the share of onsets in the last week reported by each day since onset, with 90% intervals. It stops the day before the longest delay, where the model puts all later reports. The dashed line is randomwalknowcast's share reported by age at the same release. The right panel gives the mean delay by onset week, with 90% intervals.
